# Collections Control Tower Wedge

Bharat ARP will initially serve Indian B2B distributors and light
manufacturers using ERPNext, focusing on account-level collections decisioning,
Promise-to-Pay tracking, and recovery outcomes rather than generic reminders
or a broad Agentic Resource Planning platform. This is a deliberate boundary:
ERPNext already has receivables ageing and dunning, while a narrower workflow
can be validated through paid pilots before investing in integrations,
messaging, or autonomous agents.

The validation deployment is single-tenant and local, with CSV input and no
external side effects. Revisit this decision only after three paid pilots meet
the outcome and usage criteria in `SPEC.md`; the evidence required is a baseline
comparison, at least one renewal or paid continuation, and an ERPNext partner
trial.
