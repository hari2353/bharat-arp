# Research Sources

This document records public sources used to keep the product boundary and
open-source plan grounded. It is not legal, tax, banking, or compliance advice.

## ERPNext

ERPNext documents Dunning as a traceable overdue-payment reminder linked to
Sales Invoices and Payment Entries. That supports Bharat ARP's decision not to
replace the accounting system or create a second ledger.

- https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/dunning

## Frappe Partner Ecosystem

Frappe describes a trained partner ecosystem for implementation and support.
This supports treating ERPNext partners as a validation and distribution
hypothesis rather than building a connector before paid pilot evidence.

- https://frappe.io/partners

## Account Aggregator Boundary

The RBI's Account Aggregator Directions, 2025 describe explicit consent,
customer identification, authorized sharing, and restrictions on storing or
using financial information. Bharat ARP therefore keeps Account Aggregator and
bank integrations explicitly deferred from the offline MVP.

- https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12936

## Delayed-Payment Escalation

The Ministry of MSME describes MSME Samadhaan/MSEFC delayed-payment processes
and directs new delayed-payment applications to the MSME ODR portal. Bharat ARP
does not file claims, calculate legal interest, or provide legal conclusions.

- https://samadhaan.msme.gov.in/
